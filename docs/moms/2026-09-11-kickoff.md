## Discussion Notes

### 1. Documentation & Repository Architecture
- Agreed to maintain all SRS specifications, schemas, and meeting minutes directly within the `docs/` folder in the root repository.
- Avoid external scattered wikis; every architectural update is committed and peer-reviewed via Pull Requests.

### 2. Core Functional Safeguards
- **Booth Dispatch:** Confirmed online-only terminal with atomic row locks to guarantee zero duplicate releases during high-traffic campus drops.
- **Reservation TTL:** Flash drops decrement stock immediately at order placement with a 30-minute digital / 24-hour cash TTL window.
- **Payment Deduplication:** Database-level uniqueness on payment reference numbers to prevent duplicate slip reuse across all tenants.
- **Toll-Gate:** Formula-sanitized Master Supplier Export unlocks upon 5.00 PHP/item (capped at 5%) fee clearance.

### 3. Governance Model (SRS v2.6 Update)
- `PLATFORM_OWNER` is a strict superset of `SUPERADMIN`.
- Superadmins represent managing student orgs/councils; Platform Owners retain full system visibility, account provisioning, fee exemption controls, and override authority.
